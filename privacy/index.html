Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blokowisko Planet Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with this website and with our videos published on YouTube, TikTok and other platforms.

1. Who we are

Blokowisko Planet Studio is an independent creator of animated video content. Contact: przy.korycie.studio@gmail.com.

2. This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, accounts or forms, and we do not collect personal data through it. GitHub, as hosting provider, may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs under its own privacy statement.

3. Our videos on third-party platforms

When you watch, like, comment on or share our content on YouTube, TikTok or another platform, that platform collects and processes your data under its own privacy policy. We do not control this processing. We see only the aggregated statistics and public comments that the platform provides to channel owners.

4. Information you send us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we keep the correspondence no longer than needed. We do not sell or share it with third parties.

5. TikTok

Where we post to TikTok through its tools, we use only what TikTok’s developer terms permit. We access no private data of TikTok users beyond what is needed to publish our own content.

6. Children

Our content is general-audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you may ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Write to the address above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
